## 🚨 Emergency Disclaimer
> **Important Note:** SafeZone TT does not replace official emergency services. We strongly encourage reporting to local crime authorities first. This platform serves as a secondary community effort to keep friends, family, and fellow citizens safe.

---

## 💖 Our Mission

We built **SafeZone TT** because everyone deserves to feel secure in their community. Our goal is to bridge the gap between official crime reporting and real-time community awareness. By sharing lived experiences transparently, we look out for one another, protect the vulnerable, and build a culture of vigilance and care across Trinidad and Tobago.

---

## ✨ Key Features

*   **📍 Interactive Alert Map**: Powered by Leaflet and custom dark-mode tiles, users can explore localized incidents across T&T in real time.
*   **⚠️ Caution Zones**: Automatically highlights geographic clusters where 3 or more incidents have occurred within a 5km radius.
*   **✋ "Happened to Me Too" Support**: Allows community members to validate and back incident reports to show solidarity and highlight active problem areas.
*   **🔒 Hack-Proof Data Persistence**: Secured with Supabase PostgreSQL and Row Level Security (RLS) policies to protect public integrity.
*   **⏱️ Smart Date & Time Masking**: Built-in boundary controls enforcing local time zones, preventing future-dated reports, and restricting history to a maximum 1-year window.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

*   **Framework**: Next.js (App Router)
*   **Styling**: Tailwind CSS (Dark Mode with Rose/Pink Accents)
*   **Mapping Engine**: React-Leaflet & Stadia Maps
*   **Icons**: Lucide React
*   **Database**: Supabase (PostgreSQL with RLS)
